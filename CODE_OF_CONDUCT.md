# Code of Conduct

Contributors and participants are expected to communicate respectfully, give constructive technical feedback, and welcome people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. Do not post customer logs, credentials, or other sensitive operational data.

To report a concern, contact **darisi.suresh@ieee.org**. Include links and enough context for review while avoiding unnecessary private information. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation where appropriate. Reports will be handled with care; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed where platform rules or legal obligations require disclosure.
